Before contacting support

For an inventory or backup issue, please include your app version, device model, and a description of what happened. Do not send sensitive information or your complete inventory backup unless specifically requested through an agreed secure method.

For subscription issues, you can check your subscriptions in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Purchases and refunds are managed through Apple.
